Why ownership, not a fixed return.
There is no assured or guaranteed leg here. You own a percentage of a real property - or, once it opens, of the parent company - and you are paid your share of what it actually earns, after a flat 20% management fee. That is the whole model.

Why the first payout is not year one.
A property under construction cannot pay a return, and one that just opened does not reach stabilised occupancy for a further nine to twelve months. The model applies both. Projections that skip this are the reason investors are disappointed.

Two ways in

Own a hotel, or own the chain.

Start by owning a percentage of one named property - Mewad Sanand today. As more hotels come online, own a slice of the parent company instead.
